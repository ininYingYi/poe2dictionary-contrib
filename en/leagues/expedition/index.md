# Expedition

Expedition is temporarily folded into Runes of Aldur for the 0.5.0 league,
while the old standalone Standard implementation is disabled until later
reintegration.

![Expedition](./map.webp)

## Runes of Aldur integration

- Expedition Remnants have been changed into Runes of Aldur Remnants.
- Logbooks now function as an ocean exploration mechanic.
- Using logbooks reveals ocean sections with islands to explore.
- Medved, Vorana, Uhtred, and Olroth are the four faction leaders
available on new islands.
- Defeating Olroth grants a key for the Runes of Aldur pinnacle boss.

## Temporary removals

- Expedition is temporarily disabled in Standard leagues because of the
Runes of Aldur integration.
- The Recombinator is disabled.
- Omen of Recombination was removed, and existing copies are deleted on
login.
- The Forgotten By Time Expedition Precursor Tablet is temporarily
disabled from dropping.
- Legacy Expedition currencies can be sold to vendors for Gold.

## Quality of life

- Expedition explosives now wait until most monsters from the previous
explosive are killed before detonating.
- You can use currency from your stash to buy items from Expedition
vendors.
- Several Expedition currencies were removed from the Currency Exchange.
- Old hideout versions of Rog, Gwennen, Dannig, and Tujen become
non-interactable decorations.
- Use <code>/reclaimexpeditioninventories</code> to reclaim items left
in deprecated Expedition inventories.
