# The Runes of Aldur League

**Runes of Aldur** is the v0.5.0 challenge league for Path of
Exile 2. It turns each area into a small crafting gamble: choose a Remnant
craft, add runic shapes for risk and reward, then defeat the raised
monsters to claim the result.

![Remnant](./remnant.webp)

## Core mechanic

- Each area contains a **Remnant** where you engrave Runic
Recipes into available slots.
- Remnants can roll from **2 to 10 slots**; more slots are
rarer and allow stronger crafts.
- Each extra Runeshape adds another enemy wave and more runic modifiers.
- The monsters can drop **Verisium**, the metal used for
Runeforging.
- Farrow is the new NPC who guides the league mechanic through four
campaign quests.

## Runic Ward

Runic Ward is a new defensive layer from Runeforging. It begins protecting
you after your life reaches 1, takes damage before you die, and
regenerates independently from life. Armour below item level 55 gains it
without a downside; higher-level armour trades part of its normal base
defences for Runic Ward.

## Kalguuran gems

Remnants can craft a new Kalguuran gem package: 
**23 active skills** and **7 supports**. These
skills lean into Ward costs, Ward scaling, Verisium themes, minions,
runes, and explosive transformations.

- **Active examples:** Animus Exchange, Bitter Dead,
Conductive Runes, Detonate Living, Frostflame Nova, Grim Pillars,
Hollow Shell, Rain of Blades, Runic Reprieve, Skyfall, Verisium
Manifestations, Voltaic Barrier, Wardbound Minions.
- **Support examples:** Concussive Runes, Fist of Kalguur,
Healing Runes, Runeforged Blades, Runic Extraction, Runic Infusion,
Scouring Flame.

## Expedition connection

Expedition is folded into Runes of Aldur for the league. Logbooks become
an ocean exploration system, the Runes of Aldur hub is the 
**Ruins of Kingsmarch**, and Medved, Vorana, Uhtred, and
Olroth become faction leaders on new islands. Defeating Olroth grants a
key toward the Runes of Aldur pinnacle boss.
