# Fate of the Vaal

Fate of the Vaal moved into the core game in v0.5.0, with a dedicated
Atlas tree, a new Atlas location, Temple tablets, and major Temple
cleanup.

![Fate of the Vaal](./map.webp)

## Core access

- You first encounter Fate of the Vaal through six Ancient Beacons in
Act 3.
- A second set of six Ancient Beacons appears in the Interludes.
- Energising beacons gives Energised Crystals and opens a portal to the
Vaal Ruins.
- The mechanic now has its own Fate of the Vaal Atlas Passive Tree.
- Atziri's Temple appears on the Atlas in **Lira Vaal**,
north-east of the start.

## Temple rework

- Maps inside Lira Vaal always have Energised Crystals and grant Fate of
the Vaal Atlas points.
- Temple Precursor Tablets can guarantee Vaal Beacons in maps for
focused farming.
- The old room-deletion prevention strategy is gone; required deletions
now turn connected rooms into simple path rooms.
- Temples start with more rooms, making Atziri faster to reach.
- Rooms can be upgraded to Tier 4 after unlocking the ability on the
Temple Atlas Tree.

## Item and currency notes

- Reward rooms from the Architect console were significantly improved.
- Medallion drop rate now scales with temple paths as well as other
rooms.
- The default storage cap is now 60 Energised Crystals and 6 Medallions.
- Infusers now come in Armourer's, Blacksmith's, Arcanist's, and
Catalysing versions.
- All Infusers require items at or above 20% quality.
