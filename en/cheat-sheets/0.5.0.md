# v0.5.0 Cheat Sheet

:::warning

**Limited-time launch event:** Path of Exile 2 is free to try
from **May 29, 1:00 PM PDT to June 1, 1:00 PM PDT** (Taiwan
time: **May 30, 4:00 AM to June 2, 4:00 AM**). The Early
Access Supporter Pack is also 
**50% off from May 29 to June 12**.
:::

![Path of Exile 2: Return of the Ancients infographic](https://web.poecdn.com/public/news/2026-05-22/RotAInfographic.png)

Path of Exile 2 v0.5.0, **Return of the Ancients**, is a
major Early Access update focused on the new 
**Runes of Aldur** league, a rebuilt endgame Atlas, new
endgame storylines, new ascendancies, and large balance/crafting changes.

## Start here

- **New league:** Runes of Aldur starts a fresh economy
built around Remnants, Runeshapes, Verisium, and Runeforging.
- **Endgame reset:** the Atlas gets fixed points of
interest, quests for league mechanics, quest versions of pinnacle
bosses, and 30 new map areas.
- **Atlas tree:** the Atlas passive tree expands past 300
nodes and can eventually be fully allocated through the Origins of
Divinity storyline.
- **Atlas masters:** Doryani, Hilda, and Jado add swappable
endgame progression bonuses.
- **New ascendancies:** Monk gets Martial Artist; Huntress
gets Spirit Walker.
- **Fate of the Vaal:** the previous league mechanic moves
into the core game with its own Atlas passive tree.
- **Campaign flow:** several campaign areas were shortened
or rearranged to make repeat league starts faster.

## Runes of Aldur

Check [Runes of Aldur league](/docs/community/en/leagues/aldur)

## Origins of Divinity endgame

- **New endgame storyline:** the Beast's catastrophe
triggers an ancient pact to search for corruption and purge it
completely.
- After completing your first Tower, a **Fortress** rises
from the ground. Travel through the Atlas, enter the Fortress,
activate gates, collect keys, and dismantle the ancient Precursors'
weapons.
- Fortress maps award one or more **Atlas Passive Points**,
fully replacing the previous Atlas point acquisition method.
- The Atlas passive tree now has **300+ nodes**. Completing
all maps inside the Fortress grants enough points to unlock every
Atlas passive.
- Many Fortress maps have **Ancient modifiers**, and more
than 40 Ancient modifiers can appear on maps outside the Fortress.
- The update adds Divergence maps, Precursor Tower maps, two Citadel
maps, and the new pinnacle boss **Divine Arbiter**.

## Endgame priorities

- Do the **Origins of Divinity** storyline to unlock the
rebuilt Atlas progression and Atlas passive points.
- Use the new fixed Atlas locations as progression anchors instead of
wandering randomly.
- Pick an Atlas Master setup before mapping; each master has 12 nodes,
with 4 active at once.
- Use the dedicated league pages for the deeper Delirium, Breach,
Ritual, Abyss, Expedition, and Fate of the Vaal reworks.
- Plan around the Atlas reset: old waystones/tablets still work, but new
Atlas points come from the new storyline.

## Ascendancy changes

- **New ascendancies:** Monk gets 
**Martial Artist**, while Huntress gets 
**Spirit Walker**.
- **Acolyte of Chayula:** Into the Breach now grants 20% of
maximum Life/Mana as leech from Red/Blue Flames, up from 15%.
- **Blood Mage:** Vitality Siphon now grants 20% of Spell
Damage leeched as Life, up from 10%.
- **Chronomancer:** Now and Again is reworked, Phased Form
is added, Temporal Rift is faster, and Inevitable Agony is reworked
into a life-loss debuff.
- **Gemling Legionnaire:** Crystalline Potential is
removed, Essence of Virtue is added, and Advanced Thaumaturgy now
gives socketed skills an extra quality effect.
- **Pathfinder / Witchhunter:** Pathfinder's poison and
moving-skill penalties are harsher, and Witchhunter's Obsessive
Rituals now has a larger Armour/Evasion penalty.

## Reworked league mechanics

- [Delirium](/docs/community/en/leagues/delirium): new hub, revamped tree,
Delirium bar, Grand Mirrors, Trial of Madness, and updated Simulacrum.
- [Breach](/docs/community/en/leagues/breach): new hub, Stabilised
Breaches, Genesis Tree crafting, Breach Domains, and new domain
encounters.
- [Ritual](/docs/community/en/leagues/ritual): new hub, cleaner altar
pathing, reworked rewards, Audience with the King, and Rite of the
Nameless.
- [Fate of the Vaal](/docs/community/en/leagues/fate-of-the-vaal): moves
core with its own Atlas tree, Lira Vaal, Temple tablets, Tier 4 rooms,
and Infuser changes.
- [Abyss](/docs/community/en/leagues/abyss): large Atlas cracks,
guaranteed Depths boss progression, and revamped Abyss Atlas tree.
- [Expedition](/docs/community/en/leagues/expedition): temporarily folded
into Runes of Aldur, ocean exploration, explosive pacing changes, and
vendor/currency cleanup.

## Build-impact notes

- **Leech is capped differently:** only one leech instance
per resource applies, and very large hits stop scaling leech past a
damage cap.
- **Defences wording changed:** "Defences" now explicitly
means Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield, not Runic Ward or other
protection layers.
- **Waystones changed:** waystones must be identified
before use, and their modifiers now emphasize monster effectiveness,
pack size, rarity, and waystone drops.
- **Recombinator disabled:** Expedition changes remove
Recombinator access during Runes of Aldur.
- **Trade QoL:** Shift-Alt-click can populate Trade Market
filters from an item after the May 26 patch note update.
